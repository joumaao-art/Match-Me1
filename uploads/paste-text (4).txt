Prompt 9 – Messaging, Interviews & Hiring Workflow Engine (End-to-End Hiring Journey)

Design and implement the full hiring workflow system for MatchMe, covering:

Communication (chat, notifications)
Interview scheduling & management
Hiring pipeline (ATS-lite)
Offer & hiring decision flow

This system must seamlessly connect Job Seekers, Employers, and Internal Hiring Teams.

1. SYSTEM OBJECTIVE

Build a system that:

Converts matches → conversations → interviews → hires
Provides a smooth, fast, mobile-first hiring experience
Replaces fragmented tools (email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets)

Step 5: Decision

Final Interview
