Build a modern, scalable, production-ready recruitment platform called MatchMe.

MatchMe is an AI-powered hiring platform that matches Companies and Job Seekers using a compatibility algorithm and a swipe-based experience inspired by Tinder, but designed specifically for professional recruitment.

The platform must support both:

English (default)
Arabic (RTL)

Success Criteria

The final product should be polished enough to serve as a commercial MVP that can scale into a global recruitment platform. It should prioritize exceptional UX, fast performance, secure authentication, intelligent AI-driven matching, bilingual support (English/Arabic with full RTL), and a premium experience comparable to leading platforms like LinkedIn, Tinder, and Indeed, while maintaining a unique identity centered around swipe-based professional matching.
