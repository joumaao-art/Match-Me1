Prompt 10 – Monetization, Pricing & Subscription Engine (Revenue Engine 💰)

Design and implement the full monetization system for MatchMe, covering:

Subscription plans
Pay-per-use features
Credit system

Revenue analytics

This system must be scalable, flexible, and optimized for high LTV (Lifetime Value).

1. MONETIZATION STRATEGY OVERVIEW

The platform must generate revenue from:

Primary Revenue Streams
Employer subscriptions (main revenue driver)
Pay-per-hire / success fees
Credits system (boosts, unlocks)
Premium job seeker features
Secondary Revenue Streams
Featured job listings
Employer branding pages
Talent database access
Ads (future phase)

Limited candidate views
Basic matching
No advanced analytics

More job postings
Unlimited matches

Tier 3 – Pro
Unlimited jobs

Tier 4 – Enterprise
Custom pricing
