# MATCHME — CRITICAL MVP LIVE DATA INTEGRATION
## Connect Real Company Accounts, Published Jobs and Job Seeker Swipe

### Priority: Critical — Core Marketplace Functionality

MatchMe must now operate as a real recruitment marketplace, not a simulated or demo-driven application.

I am experiencing a critical issue: I cannot find actual job cards in the running MVP, and I need jobs posted by real Company accounts to appear in the Job Seeker Swipe experience.

**Implement and verify the complete end-to-end workflow:**

Company Registration → Company Profile → Job Posting → Published Vacancy → Job Seeker Swipe → Application → Company Applicant Dashboard.

This must use actual persisted data and real user accounts.

Do not solve this by adding generated jobs, sample cards, hardcoded arrays, or fake database records.

### 1. Audit the Existing Architecture

Before changing code, inspect:

- Supabase authentication and project configuration.
- Company registration and profile creation.
- Company-to-user ownership relationships.
- Company job-posting forms.
- Vacancy database tables and relationships.
- Vacancy publication statuses.
- Supabase Row Level Security policies.
- Job Seeker profile and onboarding data.
- Swipe/Discover vacancy-fetching services.
- AI Matching and recommendation filtering.
- Applications database and Company applicant views.
- Development, preview, and production environment configuration.

Identify exactly where the real-data workflow is broken.

Determine whether the problem is caused by missing persistence, different Supabase environments, RLS, publication status, incorrect queries, matching filters, frontend state, or another issue.

Do not assume the cause without testing.

### 2. Real Company Accounts

When I create a Company account, it must be a real authenticated account in Supabase.

The Company profile must be saved persistently and linked to the authenticated user.

Requirements:

- Company profiles persist after logout and login.
- Company users can view and edit their own company profiles.
- Company logos are stored and retrievable.
- Each Company account has the correct ownership/permissions.
- Company records must not be simulated or stored only in browser state.
- Different Company accounts must remain properly isolated.

Preserve the existing mandatory Company profile fields.

### 3. Real Job Posting

When a Company creates a vacancy using Post Vacancy, the job must be saved to the canonical vacancy database.

Each vacancy must have a stable unique identifier and a valid relationship to its Company.

Persist the existing job-posting information, including where applicable:

- Position title
- Company ID
- Job description
- Qualifications
- Required skills
- Industry
- Employment type
- Employment style
- Country and City
- Salary/compensation package, if declared
- Experience requirements
- Seniority level
- Publication status
- Creation and update timestamps
- Expiration date, if applicable

Use the actual existing MatchMe data model and extend it only where necessary.

Do not create a second independent vacancy database.

### 4. Publish Jobs Properly

Implement or repair a clear vacancy lifecycle:

DRAFT → PUBLISHED → CLOSED / EXPIRED

A Company must be able to save a draft and publish a completed vacancy.

When a job is successfully published:

- It is persisted in Supabase.
- It belongs to the correct Company.
- It has a published status.
- It becomes eligible for Job Seeker discovery.
- The Company can see it in its own vacancies list.

Draft, closed, expired, deleted, or otherwise ineligible vacancies must not appear in Swipe.

If MatchMe already has a moderation or approval workflow, preserve it. Do not bypass an existing approval requirement.

### 5. Remove Generated Jobs From the Live Swipe Experience

The production/MVP Job Seeker Swipe page must retrieve real published vacancies from the database.

Do not populate the live Swipe experience with:

- AI-generated vacancies
- Hardcoded sample job arrays
- Random companies
- Placeholder job cards presented as real opportunities
- Mock API responses
- Demo fallback records

If no real eligible vacancies exist, display a professional empty state.

Example:

"No jobs available yet. Check back soon or update your preferences."

Do not invent jobs to fill the screen.

Keep any demo fixtures restricted to explicitly identified development/test environments.

### 6. Connect Company Postings to Swipe

When a Company publishes a job, it must become discoverable to eligible Job Seekers through the existing Swipe data service.

The Swipe card must display information from that exact vacancy:

- Real Company logo
- Real position title
- Actual compensation, if disclosed
- Job location
- Employment type
- Employment style
- Description
- Qualifications

The card must retain the canonical vacancy ID and Company ID.

Do not generate a separate version of the job.

Updates to the published vacancy must be reflected in Swipe after appropriate data refresh or cache invalidation.

### 7. Fix Visibility and Matching Filters

Inspect the AI Matching Engine and recommendation retrieval pipeline.

A published vacancy may be missing because filters are too restrictive or because a matching service fails.

Implement clear separation between:

**Eligibility filters**

Examples:
- Vacancy is published and active.
- Vacancy is visible to the Job Seeker.
- Vacancy has not already been applied to or passed, according to existing rules.

**Preference and ranking signals**

Examples:
- Preferred job roles
- Skills
- Employment type
- Employment style
- Seniority
- Location

Do not silently hide all published vacancies because an AI score cannot be calculated.

If AI ranking is unavailable, use a deterministic fallback ranking over eligible real vacancies, such as most recently published first.

This fallback must use real database records only.

Preserve genuinely mandatory eligibility restrictions.

### 8. Supabase Row Level Security

Audit and correct RLS policies where necessary.

Expected behavior:

- Company owners can manage their own vacancies.
- Job Seekers can read eligible published vacancies.
- Job Seekers cannot modify Company vacancy records.
- Companies cannot modify another Company's vacancies.
- Applications are accessible only to authorized parties.
- Private candidate information remains protected.

Do not disable RLS to make the MVP work.

Do not expose service-role credentials in the frontend.

### 9. One Shared Backend Environment

Verify that the Company portal and Job Seeker portal connect to the same intended Supabase project and database environment.

Check:

- Supabase project URL
- Environment variables
- Authentication configuration
- API clients
- Preview configuration
- Mobile and desktop configuration

A Company posting created in the running MVP must be visible to a Job Seeker using that same MVP environment.

Do not accidentally connect Company to development data and Job Seeker to another database.

### 10. Real Applications

When a Job Seeker swipes right or taps Heart:

- Create a real application associated with the authenticated Job Seeker and vacancy.
- Preserve the correct credit-deduction rules.
- Prevent duplicate applications.
- Update the Job Seeker Applications page.
- Make the applicant visible to the authorized Company.
- Update the Company's applicant counts.

Use the canonical Applications workflow.

Do not simulate application success without a confirmed backend result.

### 11. Real Pass, Save and Undo

Ensure these actions operate against actual vacancies:

**Pass**
- Persist the Job Seeker's Pass decision.

**Save**
- Save the real vacancy to the Job Seeker's saved jobs.

**Undo**
- Reverse the most recent eligible Pass and restore that vacancy.

These actions must remain consistent across refreshes and sessions.

### 12. Company Dashboard Integration

When a Company publishes a job:

- Active vacancy count updates.
