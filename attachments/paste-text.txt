# MATCHME — MOBILE ONBOARDING UI REFINEMENTS & CREDIT BALANCE FIX

## Objective

Implement the following adjustments to the existing MatchMe mobile Job Seeker onboarding experience.

**Important:** These are targeted corrections to the existing 11-step onboarding flow. Do not redesign or rebuild the flow, change the step sequence, reset user progress, or modify unrelated functionality.

Preserve the existing MatchMe branding and professional mobile-first design.

All Atoms Working Process updates, technical explanations, and final reports must be in **English only**. The application must continue supporting English and Arabic.

## 1. Onboarding Completion Bar — Improve Color Visibility

The current completion/progress bar colors are not sufficiently visible.

Update the progress bar to use a more distinguishable, professional color combination.

Requirements:

- Use a strong, accessible accent color consistent with the MatchMe brand for the completed portion.
- Use a clearly contrasting neutral color for the incomplete portion.
- Ensure the completed and incomplete segments are visually distinguishable on both light and dark backgrounds.
- Maintain the existing slim, modern progress bar design.
- Do not introduce gradients, glowing effects, or excessive animations.
- Ensure the progress bar updates accurately after each completed onboarding step.
- Keep the progress bar in the same position across all 11 steps.
- Do not reintroduce numerical step labels.

The result should be clean, highly visible, and consistent with a premium professional application.

## 2. Step 1 — Remove Two Fields

On Step 1, completely remove the following fields:

- Professional Headline
- Professional Summary

Remove them from the Step 1 UI and from Step 1 mandatory-field validation.

Do not delete existing professional headline or summary data from the Job Seeker database or remove these fields from other areas of the application where they are still used.

Keep all other Step 1 fields and their existing validation rules unchanged.

CV upload remains optional.

## 3. Step 2 — Remove Duplicate Gender Label

The word **Gender** currently appears twice.

Keep the main heading and remove the smaller, secondary "Gender" label above the selection options.

Preserve the three choices:

- Male
- Female
- Prefer not to say

Selection remains mandatory.

Adjust spacing after removing the duplicate label so the page looks balanced.

## 4. Step 3 — Remove Duplicate Birthday Label

Remove the smaller label:

**Birthday**

Keep the main heading:

**When is your birthday?**

Preserve the mandatory Day, Month, and Year scroll-wheel selectors.

Ensure the date selector remains centered and professionally aligned after removing the label.

## 5. Step 5 — Remove Duplicate Skills Label

Remove the smaller secondary label:

**Skills**

Keep the main heading:

**Skills**

Keep the supporting phrase:

"The more skills you add the more matches you discover"

Preserve the searchable skills dropdown, autocomplete suggestions, multiple selections, and mandatory validation.

Ensure the layout has appropriate spacing between the heading, supporting text, and selection component.

## 6. Step 6 — Fix Education Year Selection

There is currently a problem with the Education date selectors: **the years are mixed up or displayed incorrectly**.

Investigate and fix the root cause.

Requirements:

- Start Date and End Date / Expected Graduation must use consistent Month/Year scroll-wheel pickers.
- Years must appear in a logical, predictable chronological order.
- Do not mix years, repeat values, or display years in an inconsistent sequence.
- The selected year must match the year displayed and the value saved.
- Ensure scroll positioning does not select a different year from the highlighted value.
- Prevent invalid date ranges where End Date is earlier than Start Date.
- Support future years for expected graduation where appropriate.
- Support Current education without requiring a completed graduation date.
- Ensure changing the month or year does not unexpectedly reset the other selection.
- Verify correct behavior when opening, closing, reopening, and editing the picker.
- Test the selector on actual mobile devices/viewports and in Arabic RTL.

Audit year-array generation, sorting, picker indexing, controlled component state, date parsing, and timezone conversions.

**Do not fix this by merely reversing the visual order if the underlying selected and saved values remain incorrect.**

Reuse the canonical date-picker architecture so the same bug does not occur elsewhere.

## 7. Step 10 — Remove Technical Credit Explanation

Remove the following text completely from the Step 10 mobile onboarding screen:

"20 free swiping credits for eligible new accounts. Your balance follows your account across devices; returning here never grants them twice."

This text is unnecessarily technical and should not appear in the user-facing onboarding experience.

Keep the main heading:

**"Almost there…20 free swiping credits await you!"**

Maintain a clean, premium layout with the existing credit icon and Continue button.

Do not replace the removed paragraph with another technical explanation.

## 8. Step 10 — Ensure New Users Receive 20 Credits

The unified MatchMe credit policy is:

**20 free swiping credits for every eligible new Job Seeker account, on both mobile and desktop.**

Verify and correct the actual credit-awarding logic.

Requirements:

- Every eligible newly registered Job Seeker receives exactly 20 free credits.
- The initial available balance must display 20 before any credit-consuming activity.
- Credits must be granted exactly once per account.
- Mobile and desktop must share the same account credit balance.
- Refreshing the page must not award additional credits.
- Returning to Step 10 must not award additional credits.
- Logging out and back in must not award additional credits.
- Completing onboarding again must not award additional credits.
- Credit awards and deductions must use the existing centralized backend credit ledger.
- Do not create a separate mobile credit balance.
- Do not change the actual balance merely by hardcoding "20" in the interface.
- Preserve existing users' legitimately earned or purchased credits.
- Do not retroactively reduce balances belonging to existing users.

If an eligible new account has not received its initial 20-credit grant because of a signup or onboarding error, repair the missing grant safely using an idempotent backend operation.

Do not grant another 20 credits to accounts that have already received their initial entitlement.

## 9. Mobile-Only Scope

All requested layout changes must apply to the **mobile onboarding experience only**.

Do not modify the desktop onboarding layout or other desktop visual components.

The unified 20-credit entitlement and shared account balance must remain consistent across mobile and desktop.

Preserve all other onboarding fields, features, and steps.
